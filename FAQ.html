Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse the menu, add your favourite items to the cart, fill in your delivery details, choose a payment method, and click “Place Order”.

Can I edit or cancel my order?

Orders can only be edited or cancelled within a short time after placing them. If you made a mistake, contact support immediately with your order ID.

How long will delivery take?

Delivery time depends on your location, restaurant preparation time, and traffic. You will see an estimated delivery time at checkout and in your order status.

What areas do you deliver to?

Enter your address or pin code on the homepage. If your area is covered, available restaurants and delivery options will appear automatically.

What payment methods do you accept?

We support online payments (cards, UPI, wallets) and cash on delivery, depending on what is available in your area.

Is online payment safe?

Yes. All online payments are processed through secure payment gateways that encrypt your transaction details.

Do I need an account to order?

You can check out as a guest or create an account. Having an account makes it easier to track orders and save addresses.

Are there any delivery charges?

Delivery fees vary by distance, restaurant, and ongoing offers. The exact fee is always shown on the checkout page before you pay.

What if my order is late or incorrect?

Check the live order status first. If there is still an issue, contact customer support with your order ID for help with a replacement or resolution.

How can I contact customer support?

Use the “Contact Us” section or the support email/phone number shown on the website for any help with orders, payments, or technical issues.
